    What the research says

    Daily total matters most

    Muscle gains level off around 1.6 g/kg a day, with some people still benefiting up to about 2.2 g/kg. Aim higher when older or eating in a calorie deficit.

    Spread it out

    About 0.4 g/kg per meal over 4 meals works well. A big meal still builds muscle, so don't worry if one runs large.

    Pick quality sources

    Favor fish, poultry, beans, dairy and lean unprocessed red meat. Keep processed meats rare and eat oily fish about twice a week.

    Grill smarter

    Charring meat forms HCAs and PAHs. Marinate, flip often, trim fat to cut flare-ups, and skip the blackened bits.
